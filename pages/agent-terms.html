Thank you for your interest in becoming a MedVix Campus Agent. This document outlines the terms, responsibilities, earnings structure, and expectations for agents representing MedVix on campus and online. By applying and being accepted as an agent, you agree to comply with these Terms & Conditions.

1. Role & Purpose

A MedVix Campus Agent is a verified promoter who helps medical students discover and adopt MedVix. Agents are not employees; they are independent partners who earn commissions for bringing in new subscribers and for retaining existing ones.

The goal of the program is to grow MedVix through trusted peer‑to‑peer recommendations, making medical exam preparation more accessible while rewarding those who help others succeed.

2. Eligibility & Application

To become a MedVix Campus Agent you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Be a student at a recognised medical school, or have a strong network within the medical student community.
  • Complete the agent application and verification process (your identity and institution will be verified).
  • Agree to abide by these Terms & Conditions.

Important:

  • Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance into the Agent Program.
  • MedVix reserves the right to approve or reject any application at its sole discretion.
  • MedVix may request additional information or documentation to verify your eligibility.
  • Agents are not required to have an active MedVix subscription, but they are encouraged to use the app so they can speak authentically about its benefits.

3. Earning Structure

Agents earn commissions based on the subscriptions they refer:

  • New subscriber (first paid subscription) → KSh 40 per new user
  • Renewal (subsequent paid subscriptions) → KSh 15 per renewal

Commission Validation – Important

A commission is considered earned only after ALL of the following conditions are met:

  1. The subscription payment has been successfully received and verified by MedVix.
  2. The payment is not refunded, reversed, charged back, or cancelled.
  3. The subscriber's account is not found to be fraudulent, duplicate, or in violation of MedVix's Terms of Service.
  4. The subscriber's account remains in good standing for at least 7 days after the initial payment (to prevent chargeback fraud).

Agents acknowledge that:

  • Commissions are provisional until the above conditions are satisfied.
  • MedVix may reverse or withhold commissions arising from refunded, reversed, fraudulent, duplicate, or disputed transactions.
  • MedVix's systems will automatically adjust the agent's wallet if a refund or chargeback occurs on a referred subscription.

4. Referral Attribution

How Referrals Are Credited

  • A referral is attributed according to MedVix's referral tracking system.
  • When a new user clicks an agent's referral link, the system records the agent's referral code.
  • The referral is credited only when the new user completes their first paid subscription.
  • Where multiple referral links are used (e.g., a user clicks multiple links), the referral will be credited based on the system's recorded attribution (the first link clicked and recorded).

MedVix's Records Are Final

MedVix's referral records shall be final and conclusive. In the event of a dispute regarding referral attribution, MedVix's system logs shall prevail.

No Commission on Self-Referrals

Agents are not entitled to commissions for:

  • Their own subscriptions.
  • Accounts owned or controlled by them.
  • Family members or friends created solely to generate commissions.
  • Duplicate or multiple accounts created by the same individual.

5. Prohibited Activities

Agents are strictly prohibited from:

Misrepresentation

  • Misrepresenting MedVix's features, pricing, benefits, or affiliation.
  • Making false or misleading claims about the app or its competitors.

Spam & Harassment

  • Sending unsolicited bulk messages (spam) via email, SMS, WhatsApp, or any other channel.
  • Harassing, pressuring, or coercing potential users.

Fraudulent Activities

  • Creating fake accounts, using bots, or employing automated scripts to generate artificial referrals.
  • Using referral links on coupon websites, cashback sites, automated advertising platforms, or any platform intended solely to exploit referral rewards (referral link abuse).
  • Using artificial intelligence, bots, fake traffic, or any artificial methods intended to generate referrals.
  • Engaging in any activity that violates applicable laws or regulations.

Brand Usage

  • Agents may only use official MedVix logos, posters, and promotional materials provided by MedVix.
  • Modification of branding or creating misleading advertisements is strictly prohibited.
  • Agents may not imply any formal affiliation, endorsement, or employment relationship with MedVix beyond the Agent Program.

Competitor Promotion

  • Agents may not simultaneously promote competing medical exam preparation platforms in a way that conflicts with MedVix's interests.
  • Agents must not use their MedVix agent status to promote competing products.

Violation of these prohibitions may result in immediate termination of the agent relationship and forfeiture of all commissions.

6. Wallet & Withdrawals

Wallet

  • All commissions appear in your Agent Wallet.
  • You can view your balance, referral history, and withdrawal status in the Referral Dashboard (inside the MedVix app).
  • Commissions are posted to your wallet after they are earned (as defined in Section 3).

Withdrawals

  • Minimum withdrawal: KSh 100.
  • Withdrawal requests can be submitted via the app (you must provide a valid M‑Pesa phone number).
  • Withdrawals are processed manually in the initial phase (within 48 hours) and will later be automated via M‑Pesa (Daraja B2C).
  • Identity verification may be required before processing withdrawals, particularly for withdrawals exceeding KSh 1,000 or for agents with high referral volumes.

Payment Errors

  • If commissions are paid in error due to a system fault, accounting mistake, or fraud, MedVix reserves the right to recover or deduct the incorrect amount from future commissions.
  • If your wallet balance is negative (due to refunds or chargebacks), MedVix reserves the right to recover the shortfall from future commissions or by other lawful means.

7. Fraud Investigations

MedVix takes fraud prevention seriously.

Right to Investigate

  • MedVix reserves the right to delay withdrawals while investigating suspicious activity.
  • During an investigation, commissions may be temporarily withheld.
  • MedVix may request additional information from the agent to assist with the investigation.

Investigation Triggers

Suspicious activity may include, but is not limited to:

  • Unusual referral patterns (e.g., a large number of referrals in a short period).
  • Multiple referrals from the same IP address or device.
  • Referrals from duplicate or suspicious accounts.
  • Complaints from referred users about being misled.

Consequences

  • If fraud is confirmed, MedVix may terminate the agent relationship.
  • All unpaid commissions may be forfeited.
  • MedVix may take legal action if warranted.

8. Head Agents

MedVix may appoint Head Agents with additional responsibilities and a separate commission structure.

  • Head Agents may oversee regional agent networks.
  • Head Agents may receive additional compensation (e.g., a percentage of commissions earned by agents in their network).
  • Appointment and removal of Head Agents is at MedVix's sole discretion.
  • The specific terms for Head Agents will be set out in a separate agreement.

9. Non‑Exclusivity & Conflicts of Interest

  • Participation in the Agent Program is non‑exclusive.
  • Agents remain free to participate in other lawful activities, provided they do not misrepresent MedVix or create conflicts of interest.
  • Agents must not use their MedVix agent status to promote competing products.
  • If a conflict of interest arises, the agent must disclose it to MedVix promptly.

10. Termination & Suspension

Grounds for Termination

MedVix may suspend or terminate an agent's participation in the program where it reasonably believes:

  • These Terms have been violated.
  • Continued participation may pose a risk to the Agent Program, MedVix, or its users.
  • The agent has engaged in fraudulent, abusive, or misleading conduct.

Process

  • MedVix will provide notice of termination (where feasible).
  • Unpaid commissions that are already earned (as defined in Section 3) will be paid in the next withdrawal cycle, provided the agent is not terminated for fraud or violation of these Terms.
  • If terminated for cause, all unpaid commissions may be forfeited.

Voluntary Withdrawal

  • Agents may withdraw from the program at any time by notifying MedVix.
  • Commissions already earned but not yet paid will be paid in the next withdrawal cycle (provided minimum balance is met).

  • Where required by law, MedVix may deduct applicable taxes before payment.
  • Agents may be required to provide tax identification information for compliance purposes.
  • MedVix will issue payment records that agents may use for tax reporting.

12. Limitation of Liability

  • MedVix provides the Agent Program "as is" and does not guarantee any specific income.
  • MedVix is not responsible for any losses, damages, or expenses incurred by agents in connection with their promotional activities.
  • Agents are independent partners and are solely responsible for their own taxes, expenses, and legal compliance related to their earnings.
  • In no event shall MedVix's liability to an agent exceed the total commissions paid to that agent in the preceding 12 months.

13. Data Protection & Privacy

  • MedVix collects and processes agent data (name, contact details, institution, referral statistics) in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
  • We will not share your personal data with third parties except as required to process payments, to comply with legal obligations, or as specified in our Privacy Policy.
  • Agents must handle referral data (e.g., emails, phone numbers) with care and not share it with unauthorised parties.
  • Agents shall comply with applicable data protection laws when collecting or using personal data of potential referrals.
